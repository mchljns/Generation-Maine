### Headline options

1. **Young Mainers on building a life here** (used)
2. Staying in Maine, told by the people trying
3. Maine's next generation, telling its own story

I used option 1. It says what the project is and who makes it in seven words. It names Maine and young people without taking a side on any policy. It also works as the brand line on the YouTube banner, share card and channel bios, so the message stays the same everywhere. Option 2 is more emotional but assumes every creator is struggling to stay. Option 3 is broad and could describe almost any youth project.

## What is Generation Maine

- **Eyebrow:** What is Generation Maine
- **Heading:** A storytelling project made by young Mainers

Generation Maine is a group of 8 to 12 young content creators from across the state. Each one makes short videos about their own life in Maine.

The videos show how economic rules affect everyday choices. Some are about finding a place to live. Others are about getting a job, starting a business or deciding whether to stay.

The creators tell their own stories. You can watch them on social media and read more on our Substack.
