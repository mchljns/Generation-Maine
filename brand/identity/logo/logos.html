Signature

Spruce and Marigold. Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold. The mark is a field of nine lines that part for a room at the margin and turn gold where they bend. The wordmark keeps its dot only when it stands alone, so Marigold appears once.

Clear space on every side is the radius of the room, one fifth of the mark's height. Minimum size: mark 24 px, horizontal lockup 140 px wide, endorsed lockup 220 px wide. Below 110 px the six-line version is used.

Bark & Sky

Bark, Sky and Clay. Hedvig Letters, all lowercase. The same rule drawn quieter: seven thin lines of one weight, the bends in Clay on paper and in white on Sky or Bark. Lockups centre, the way this concept sets everything.
