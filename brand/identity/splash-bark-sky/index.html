Young Mainers on building a life here

Young Mainers film the rules that shape their lives. What rent costs, what a license costs, what it takes to stay. Told from the towns they live in.

Watch the storiesGet the newsletter

Katahdin, Aroostook County, Cadillac Mountain, Portland Head Light, the Old Port. Photographs via Wikimedia Commons, credits in the footer.

Made by the people it is about

Who makes it

Young Mainers with a phone and a story. They pick what to film and say it their own way.

What it is about

The rules behind everyday costs. Leases, licenses, permits, wages, and the fine print nobody reads until it costs them.

Where to find it

Short videos on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. The full story, with the numbers, by email.

The creators

Nine young Mainers in nine towns. Each one films where they live and says it their own way. Names and faces arrive after the shoot.

@maya.skowMaya

Skowhegan, Maine

Maya

I grew up here and came back after two years in Portland. I work the front desk at a dental office and film on my lunch break. My first video was about the three apartments in town I could look at and the one I could afford.

@devinpourscoffeeDevin

Presque Isle, Maine

Devin

I wanted to sell coffee from a cart outside the hospital. It took eleven signatures and most of a winter. I make videos about what each form was for, in the order I had to get them.

@devinpourscoffee@devinpourscoffee@devinpourscoffee
@priya.biddoPriya

Biddeford, Maine

Priya

Half of my graduating class left Maine. I stayed and started asking the others why. I film the conversations on the mill walk by the river, one person at a time.

@priya.biddo@priya.biddo@priya.biddo
@cole.drivesCole

Machias, Maine

Cole

Forty minutes each way to a job that pays the rent, barely. I mount the phone on the dash and talk through the math while I drive. The numbers are real and so is the gas.

@cole.drives@cole.drives@cole.drives
@jess.backhomeJess

Lewiston, Maine

Jess

I moved back in with my parents at 26. I have a degree and a full-time job and a spreadsheet that explains the rest. My videos walk through one row of it at a time.

@jess.backhome@jess.backhome@jess.backhome
@tyler.rumfordTyler

Rumford, Maine

Tyler

First lease, first security deposit, first surprise fee. I am 21 and learning the rules by hitting them. I keep every receipt and read them out loud on camera.

@tyler.rumford@tyler.rumford@tyler.rumford
@amara.twojobsAmara

Belfast, Maine

Amara

One job pays for the room. The second one pays for everything else. I film between shifts, usually in the car, and I keep it to the parts people never say out loud.

@amara.twojobs@amara.twojobs@amara.twojobs
@noah.rnNoah

Fort Kent, Maine

Noah

I am finishing a nursing license and adding up what it costs before the first paycheck: exams, fees, background checks, gas to Presque Isle. I show the receipts, not the speech.

@noah.rn@noah.rn@noah.rn
@sam.sanfordSam

Sanford, Maine

Sam

Landscaping stops in November. I film what I do until April: plowing, odd jobs, the weeks with nothing. The off season is the part nobody budgets for, so I do it on camera.
