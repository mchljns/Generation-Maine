01 / The idea

Young Mainers tell it from here.

Generation Maine pairs short videos with the detail behind them. Young Mainers choose what matters, film it where they live, and speak in their own voice.

A lease, a license, a paycheck, a move home. The everyday decisions tell us more when we can see the place and hear the person making them.
