the cairn, drawn from the photographs

What the references say: a real cairn is either a rough heap of many stones, or a built structure of a few big ones. Each form below is drawn from a named photograph, shown in Bark on Paper, with the top stone in Sky on Bark, and as the avatar at 110, 40 and 16 px. Reference photographs are study material only and are credited in REFERENCES.md.

the Bates cairn

Acadia's own design, drawn from the Gorham Mountain photographs: two rounded granite boulders, a split slab across them, a pointer stone set toward the way. A structure, not a pile. Only Maine builds these, and the park maintains them.
