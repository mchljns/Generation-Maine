wedges, varied and standing up

Every stone its own shape, and a stack that obeys gravity: each stone rests on the one below at two points, its center of mass sits over the bearing surface under it, and the round stone sits in the dip of the top wedge. The balance line under each build is the numeric check: the center of each stone against the span it rests on.
