round five: shapes with meaning, built for 16 px

The cairn is out. Each idea here is one or two shapes with a reason to exist, shown in Bark on Paper and Sky on Bark, then as the avatar at 110, 40 and 16 in both, and beside the wordmark.

the blaze

One painted rectangle on a tree, the way a trail is marked in Maine. Sky on Bark is the name of the direction, literally. Means: you are on the path, keep going. Reads at any size.

generation maine

the double blaze

Two blazes offset. On the trail it means a turn ahead. A second form for free.

generation maine

first light

The sun's edge over the ground line. The first sunrise in the United States lands on Maine, on Cadillac and at West Quoddy. A new generation and a new day, in the ground and sky disc.

generation maine

first light, as a line

Only the sun's edge, a thin arc on the ground. Quieter, closer to the serif.

generation maine

Katahdin

The flat top and the Knife Edge as the line between ground and sky. Every Mainer knows the profile. The hero loop opens on it.

generation maine

Katahdin, as a line

The same profile drawn as one line, in the disc.

the lit window

One window in a small town with one light on. Somebody stayed. Risk: a two by two grid is a software logo.

generation maine

the fine print

A sheet with a torn edge and three short lines. The rules nobody reads until they cost them. Risk: it is an icon more than a mark.

generation maine

the threshold

A doorway, half open, with the sky through it. Building a life here starts with a door and a lease. Risk: real estate.
