The lockups, Bark & Sky

The same drawing in the second concept: Bark on Paper, Sky on Bark, the lowercase Hedvig name, one weight, no dot. Each row shows color on Paper and reversed on Bark.

1. Two-line. The primary lockup. The state beside the name on two lines, as tall as both

2. Horizontal, mid cut. For short bars: the nav, the video bug, the masthead strip. A large version with the full cut exists for 600 px wide and up

3. Compact. The mid cut inside the cap height, for bylines and footers

4. Horizontal, state after the name. In the files, not recommended

5. Stacked left. The state over the two-line name, for narrow spaces

6. Stacked centered. The state over the one-line name, for title cards and print

7. Endorsed. The horizontal lockup with the disclosure line

8. Endorsed, stacked. For the end card and the back of print

9. The wordmark alone

At working sizes. Horizontal at 480, 300 and 160 px wide. Compact at 100 px. Marks at 110, 40 and 16 px.
