Where the marigold goes

Marigold on a light field is 1.7:1 as text, and no yellow reaches 4.5:1 on Paper. So the rule: on light fields the second word is blue and marigold appears only as the mark's stripe; on navy and blue the second word is marigold. Title case left, uppercase right.

On Paper

Navy name, Maine in blue (6.6:1), marigold only in the stripe.

On white

Same rule.

On navy

White name, Maine in marigold (7.4:1).

On blue

White name, Maine in marigold (3.9:1, passes for large type; the lockup is always large).

On Paper, one color

No accent at all. Print, embroidery, anywhere one color is all there is.
