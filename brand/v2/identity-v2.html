Generation Maine · Identity v2 · September 2026

From a logo to a system.

Version 1 set the name in a nice typeface and added an orange dot. Version 2 gives each direction a real brandmark with an idea inside it, a type pairing with range, and a set of rules a small team can repeat. v1 is saved in brand/archive/v1.

A real brandmark

A symbol that means something, works at 16 px, and can move.

Typographic contrast

A precise grotesk paired with an expressive second voice. Fewer weights, more restraint.

Repeatable devices

One or two graphic devices used the same way everywhere. That consistency is what reads as premium.

Direction A, v2

First Light

Maine is known as one of the first places in the country to see the sunrise (to verify: Cadillac Mountain, part of the year). The brandmark is a G whose open mouth holds a rising sun. The crossbar is the horizon. The sun is also the red light on a camera that says it is recording.

A new generation, just coming up. Stories, recorded as they happen.

The brandmark

A letter, a horizon, a record light.

Built from a circle, one straight bar and one dot. No gradients and no fine detail, so it holds at 16 px and on a phone screen at arm's length.

The G stays green or white. The sun is always Signal orange in color versions. Nothing else in the system uses a filled orange circle, so the dot always points back to the brand.

The horizon rule

One thin line with the sun sitting on it. It divides sections, underlines titles and ends every video. Move the dot along the line to show progress through a series.

Direction B, v2

Postmark

Every story is sent from somewhere. The brandmark is a postmark with "ME" at its center: the postal code for Maine, and the first-person voice of every creator. Cancellation lines run off the edge like sound waves.

It turns into a system on its own. Each creator gets a postmark with their town in the center.

The brandmark

Posted from Maine

Ring text in Anton: GENERATION MAINE across the top, YOUNG MAINERS across the bottom. A single ink color, like a real rubber stamp. At small sizes it drops the ring text and keeps the circle and ME.

Lockup and icon

Creator postmarks

One stamp per town

Town names are examples only. Each creator's postmark carries their hometown and the month they joined. It goes on their lower third, their profile card and their first video.

Applications

Stamp, tape, type

Three devices: the postmark, a strip of yellow label tape behind the key words, and perforated stamp edges around photos. Anton for headlines, IBM Plex Mono for everything else.

Recommendation

Lead with First Light.

It has the strongest single idea, it reads as polished next to a policy institute's name, and it stays calm enough to let the creators' faces lead. Postmark is the more playful option and has the best built-in system for featuring each creator. Its creator stamps could also live inside First Light as a campaign device.
