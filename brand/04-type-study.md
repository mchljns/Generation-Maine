# Type study: Google Fonts

A search of the full Google Fonts library for a typeface that fits Generation Maine. Done September 30, 2026, from the google/fonts repository on GitHub.

Specimens: `brand/type-study/specimens.png`. Each card shows the wordmark, a cover, a name and town line, body text, and the avatar G at 64 and 32 px. The sample name, Émilie Thériault of Madawaska, is for layout only.

## What the type has to do

1. Set big and tight on covers, and still read as a small G in a 32 px avatar.
2. Feel native on TikTok and credible to an older Substack reader.
3. Carry French and Acadian names, such as Thériault, Lévesque and Côté, with the right accents.
4. Stay clear of Green Falls (Archivo, Libre Franklin), corporate house fonts and faces that date fast.
5. Be free to use everywhere under the Open Font License, with a real bold or heavier.

## How we narrowed it

**Cut on sight:**
- Tied to Green Falls: Archivo, Libre Franklin.
- Company house fonts: TikTok Sans, Reddit Sans, Mona Sans, Hubot Sans, Geist, Wix Madefor, Zalando Sans.
- Defaults that make a brand look like everyone: Inter, Montserrat, Poppins, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato, DM Sans.
- Place or era baggage: Big Shoulders (Chicago), Besley and other Clarendons (Western and Americana), Cinzel (classical).
- Luxury and fashion: Playfair, Bodoni Moda.
- Already used in earlier rounds: Bricolage, Fraunces, Instrument, Anton.

## Recommendations

**1. Schibsted Grotesk, for headlines and text. Best all-rounder.**
Bakken & Bæck drew it for Schibsted, a large Scandinavian news publisher, to work in apps. It is punchy at 800 and 900 on covers, calm in body text and credible to a news reader. The G holds at 32 px. It is less quirky than Bricolage, and far less common on startup sites. One family does everything, which keeps Canva simple.

**2. Epunda Slab with Epunda Sans. Most distinctive.**
New in 2025 from Typofactur. The slab has the authority of a local paper without the Western look of a Clarendon. The sans is the tightest face we measured, so long titles fit. It is the warmest pairing and the least seen. It is also the newest, so Canva almost certainly needs an upload.

**3. Funnel Display with Funnel Sans. Youngest feel.**
Its small cut-in details give it energy on TikTok. The risk is that details like these date faster than a plain grotesque.

**Body text in any pairing: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next.** The Braille Institute drew it for readers with low vision. It is a real accessibility choice, and it fits a project that promises to be open about who is behind it.

**For Bark & Sky:** swap Fraunces, which is now very common, for Newsreader. It is an editorial serif with sizes tuned for headlines and text.

**Set aside:**
- Neutral and fine, but not ours: Gabarito, Hanken Grotesk, Figtree, Onest, Rethink Sans.
- Public Sans reads as federal government. It is the typeface of the US Web Design System.
- Parkinsans and Geologica are too wide and too playful for the older reader.
- Familjen Grotesk stops at 700.
- Young Serif has only one weight.
