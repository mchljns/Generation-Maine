01 What it is

A storytelling project made by young Mainers

Generation Maine is a group of 8 to 12 young content creators from across the state. Each one makes short videos about their own life in Maine.

The videos show how economic rules affect everyday choices. Some are about finding a place to live. Others are about getting a job, starting a business or deciding whether to stay.

The creators tell their own stories. You can watch them on social media and read more on our Substack.
